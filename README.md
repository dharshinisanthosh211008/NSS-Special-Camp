            <h2>1. Introduction to NSS Special Camp</h2>

            <p>
                The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a student-oriented
                public service programme in India. It encourages students
                to participate in community service and develop a sense
                of social responsibility.
            </p>

            <p>
                An NSS Special Camp is an important part of NSS activities.
                Students and volunteers spend a period of time in a village
                or community to understand people's needs and contribute
                to local development. The camp usually includes social
                awareness programmes, cleanliness activities, health
                education, environmental protection and cultural events.
            </p>

            <p>
                The main idea of NSS is expressed through its motto:
                <strong>"Not Me, But You."</strong> This motto teaches
                students to think about the welfare of others and work
                together for the benefit of society.
            </p>

            <div class="highlight">
                <strong>Theme:</strong> Community service and village development
                <br>
                <strong>Participants:</strong> NSS volunteers, students,
                programme officers and community members
                <br>
                <strong>Purpose:</strong> To learn through service and
                contribute to the welfare of the village.
            </div>

            <h3>Objectives of the NSS Special Camp</h3>

            <ul>
                <li>To develop social responsibility among students.</li>
                <li>To understand the needs and problems of rural communities.</li>
                <li>To promote cleanliness and environmental protection.</li>
                <li>To create awareness about health, education and hygiene.</li>
                <li>To develop leadership, teamwork and communication skills.</li>
                <li>To encourage students to participate in community development.</li>
                <li>To build a relationship between educational institutions
                    and local communities.</li>
            </ul>

            <h3>Importance of the Special Camp</h3>

            <p>
                The camp provides practical learning outside the classroom.
                Students can understand social issues by interacting with
                villagers and taking part in community activities. They learn
                the importance of cooperation, discipline, respect and
                responsible citizenship.
            </p>

            <p>
                Through these experiences, students become more aware of
                the challenges faced by society and learn how small,
                collective efforts can bring positive changes.
            </p>
        </section>

        <!-- PAGE 3: VILLAGE PROGRAMME -->
        <section id="village">
            <h2>3. NSS Village Programme</h2>

            <p>
                A village programme is an important component of an NSS
                Special Camp. It focuses on understanding the local
                community and supporting activities that improve the
                quality of village life.
            </p>

            <p>
                Volunteers can interact with residents, local leaders,
                teachers and community organisations to understand
                village needs. Activities should be planned respectfully
                and in consultation with the community.
            </p>

            <h3>Major Components of a Village Programme</h3>

            <ol>
                <li>
                    <strong>Village Survey:</strong>
                    Volunteers may gather basic information about
                    education, sanitation, drinking water, public
                    facilities and community needs, with appropriate
                    permission and respect for privacy.
                </li>

                <li>
                    <strong>Sanitation and Cleanliness:</strong>
                    Conduct cleanliness awareness activities and
                    encourage proper waste segregation and disposal.
                </li>

                <li>
                    <strong>Education:</strong>
                    Organise reading sessions, educational games and
                    learning support for school children.
                </li>

                <li>
                    <strong>Health Awareness:</strong>
                    Share general information about hygiene, nutrition
                    and healthy practices. Medical activities should
                    involve qualified health professionals.
                </li>

                <li>
                    <strong>Environmental Protection:</strong>
                    Promote tree care, water conservation and protection
                    of local natural resources.
                </li>

                <li>
                    <strong>Community Participation:</strong>
                    Encourage residents to take part in discussions,
                    cultural programmes and local improvement activities.
                </li>
            </ol>

            <h3>Benefits of the Village Programme</h3>

            <p>
                The village programme benefits both the community
                and the student volunteers. Community members gain
                awareness about important social and environmental
                issues. Students gain practical experience and a
                better understanding of rural life.
            </p>

            <ul>
                <li>Improves awareness of cleanliness and hygiene.</li>
                <li>Encourages environmental responsibility.</li>
                <li>Supports educational and awareness activities.</li>
                <li>Builds cooperation between students and villagers.</li>
                <li>Develops leadership and social service skills.</li>
                <li>Encourages students to become responsible citizens.</li>
            </ul>

            <h3>Expected Outcomes</h3>

            <p>
                The expected outcomes of an NSS Special Camp include
                greater community awareness, active student participation,
                improved understanding of local needs and stronger
                relationships between the college and village.
            </p>

            <p>
                The long-term success of a village programme depends
                on continued cooperation. Volunteers should respect
                local customs, follow safety guidelines and work
                with community members rather than making promises
                that the camp cannot fulfil.
            </p>
        </section>
